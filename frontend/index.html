INFO

Why "Crew Chiefs" instead of individual referees?

Each NFL game has 7 officials, but penalty data only tells us that a flag was thrown - not who threw it. We track Crew Chiefs (head referees) because they set the standard for their crew and are publicly accountable. This is the same method used by Vegas oddsmakers and professional analysts.
